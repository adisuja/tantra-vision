    The thinking behind it

    Teams think they have a lead problem

    They have a revenue engine problem. A team runs a cold campaign or posts on LinkedIn, books a few calls and calls it lead generation. Most people leak out before they buy, at six points: traffic, landing and sign up, reminders and nurture, booking the call, the sales pipeline, and the 52 weeks after. Fixing it today means, by our estimate, 8 to 12 tools, an agency at $10,000 or more, or months of learning.

    GrowthClub, our agency, has worked with 200+ brands over 7 years (case study deck). Growth Idea went from 9 to 25 booked calls across four webinar tests. Fintech Circle's March 2026 webinar reached 20,286 people, drew 114 registrations and 117 attendees, and booked 9 calls with no SDR team: 7.7 calls per 100 attendees on a warm, board level list. The model uses the lower 2025 floor of 2.7.

    Every stage has a tool. Nobody runs the funnel.

    Instantly, Smartlead, lemlist and HeyReach send messages. ClickFunnels and site builders make pages. Zoom and WebinarGeek run events. HubSpot, Pipedrive and GoHighLevel store deals. GoHighLevel (over 120,000 customers) and HubSpot cover every stage, but leave the design and the fixing to you. HubSpot and Salesforce are adding agents (Breeze, Agentforce); we win with teams that have no RevOps person to run them.

    What is hard to copy

    • Funnel know-how, encoded. Seven years of what works at each stage for each kind of business.
    • The funnel equations and the experiment loop. One ratio per stage, and an agent that ships a better version of the weakest element every week.
    • Payment attribution. Tying revenue back to the funnel makes revenue share pricing possible.
    • Data that compounds. Every customer funnel adds stage benchmarks. Today it rests on 9 to 10 client funnels and 23 webinars of our own.

    The outreach engine on its own can be copied. The moat is everything above it.

    What we will not do

    • Take done for you service clients. Enterprise is done with you, with a year on the platform.
    • Sell WhatsApp as cold outreach. It stays for reminders and nurture to people who opted in.
    • Use the masterclass to win customers. The workshop series runs from 28 December as a small test and grows only once the two webinars are on plan.
    • Sell to B2C brands, or build a feature no paying customer asked for.
